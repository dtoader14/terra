NASA MODIS / Collection 6.1
DATA & METHODOLOGY

Observations, made visible.

This app uses NASA AppEEARS to retrieve MODIS Collection 6.1 products from the Terra and Aqua satellites: land-surface temperature (MOD11A2, MYD11A2), vegetation indices (MOD13A2, MYD13A2), leaf area index and FPAR (MOD15A2H), plant growth (MOD17A2HGF) and evapotranspiration (MOD16A2GF). The list shows the variables that have data in your dates.

What the colors mean

Colors represent the variable's physical value. Temperature, vegetation indices, leaf area and FPAR are satellite retrievals. Evapotranspiration and plant growth are NASA estimates computed from satellite and weather-model data. None is a forecast. Clouds, missing observations, and pixels that fail NASA's quality flags stay transparent.

A careful view of time

Vegetation indices span 16 days; the other products span 8. The period map is an equally weighted mean of available composites, not a daily or duration-weighted average. Composite start dates identify frames; their observation windows may extend beyond your dates.

Display resolution

Sources are approximately 1 km or 500 m. For a responsive map, the app samples a grid of up to 640 pixels per side using nearest neighbors. GeoTIFF exports use that same Web Mercator grid. Statistics describe displayed pixels, not an area-weighted regional mean. Metadata includes the NASA task ID, source filenames, checksums, quality rules, and scale factors.

Time series

Monthly values for Romania from NASA Giovanni and NASA POWER. Each variable says whether it is a satellite measurement or a model product. A town is the grid cell that contains it; a box is the mean of the grid cells inside it, weighted by area; a county is the box around its towns, not its exact outline. The trend is a straight line through the yearly means of complete years, with the p-value of its slope and the Mann-Kendall test beside it.

Heat maps come through AppEEARS; time series through Giovanni and POWER. NOAA CDO is a reference link only. Town names: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0.
