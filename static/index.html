NASA MODIS / Collection 6.1
DATA & METHODOLOGY

Observations, made visible.

This app uses NASA AppEEARS to retrieve MODIS Terra Collection 6.1 satellite products: land-surface temperature (MOD11A2) and vegetation indices (MOD13A2).

What the colors mean

Colors represent physical temperature or a vegetation index. Values are satellite retrievals and composites, not forecasts. Clouds, missing observations, and pixels that fail the quality checks stay transparent.

A careful view of time

Temperature products span 8 days; vegetation products span 16. The period map is an equally weighted mean of available composites, not a daily or duration-weighted average. Composite start dates identify frames; their observation windows may extend beyond your dates.

Display resolution

Sources are approximately 1 km. For a responsive map, the app samples a grid of up to 640 pixels per side using nearest neighbors. GeoTIFF exports use that same Web Mercator grid. Statistics describe displayed pixels, not an area-weighted regional mean. Metadata includes the NASA task ID, source filenames, checksums, quality rules, and scale factors.
